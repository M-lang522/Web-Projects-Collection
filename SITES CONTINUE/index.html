About the Studio

We don't just animate. We orchestrate experiences.

VÉLOCE is an independent motion design studio partnering with ambitious brands to create digital products that feel alive. From micro-interactions to full-page transitions, we obsess over the details that make users smile.

Our work sits at the intersection of design engineering and visual storytelling — where every scroll, hover, and tap is an opportunity to delight.

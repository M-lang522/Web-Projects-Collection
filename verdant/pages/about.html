STEEPING…
Founded 2021, Kyoto

We started in a rented lab bench, obsessed with one question: why does extraction temperature change everything?

Verdant Lab began as a side experiment between a formulation chemist and a botanist tired of skincare that treated plants as marketing, not chemistry.

FIRST BATCH — MARCH 2021
The Timeline

Five years, one obsession, still unfinished.

2021

A Rented Bench

Two founders, one cold-extraction rig, and a single plot of shiso leaf. The first batch yielded eleven vials — none of them sold.

2022

The Spectrometer

We bought our first UV-Vis spectrometer secondhand, finally able to measure what we'd only been guessing at for a year.

2023

Portland Studio Opens

A second extraction site, chosen for its climate — cooler, wetter, better suited to the leafy botanicals we'd started sourcing.

2024

Published INCI Standard

We committed to listing extraction dates and origin plots on every label, a first for the category at our scale.

2026

Batch 2026.07

Our fourteenth consecutive quarter of single-origin sourcing, now serving consult clients across two continents.

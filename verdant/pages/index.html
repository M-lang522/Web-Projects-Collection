The Extraction Timeline

From leaf to
vial in five stages.

Scroll to move through our process — the same sequence every batch undergoes before it reaches a consult.

STAGE 01

Harvest

Leaves are cut at dawn, when chlorophyll concentration peaks before photosynthetic decline.

STAGE 02

Cold Extraction

Plant matter is macerated in a low-temperature solvent bath for 14 hours.

STAGE 03

Filtration

Triple-filtered through cellulose to isolate active compounds from raw cellulose fiber.

STAGE 04

Spectrometry

Each batch is measured for active concentration and logged against our purity threshold.

STAGE 05

Vialing

Extract is hand-poured into violet glass and sealed within the hour of testing.

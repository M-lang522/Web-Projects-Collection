The Story

A drum, a notebook, and one stubborn question.

Emberwood began as a question our founder couldn't put down: why did the coffee taste different every time, even from the same farm? Three years of failed batches later, the answer turned out to be patience.

Every lot we roast is logged by hand — bean temperature, ambient humidity, the sound of first crack — then cupped blind against the batch before it. If it doesn't beat the last one, it doesn't leave the room.

The Roast

Four decisions we don't compromise on.

Direct Origin

We buy six lots a year, in person, at prices set before the harvest — not after the market moves.

Avg. altitude1,940m

Small Drums

Twelve kilograms per batch — small enough that one roaster can taste the difference a single degree makes.

Batch size12kg

Cupped Blind

Three cuppers score every lot without seeing the roast log first. Anything under 86 gets sent back to the drum.

Avg. cupping score87.5

Roasted To Ship

Beans leave the roastery within 48 hours of roasting — never before, so the rest gas has time to settle.
